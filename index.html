🧐 About Me

I am Xu Binbin (徐彬彬), a Ph.D. student in Management Science and Engineering at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Institutes of Science and Development (GPA: 3.9/4), where I have been pursuing my doctoral degree since September 2022. In May 2026, I was awarded a China Scholarship Council (CSC) fellowship to pursue a joint-training Ph.D. in Computer Science at the College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS), Nanyang Technological University (NTU), focusing on generative AI agent construction. Prior to this, I received my B.S. in Business Administration from Southwest Jiaotong University, School of Economics and Management (Chengdu, China) in June 2022.

My research focuses on applying advanced AI and data-driven methods — including NLP, knowledge graph construction, graph neural networks, and large language models — to problems in generative agents, energy strategy, technology intelligence, and scholarly influence evaluation.

🤔 Research Interests:

  • Generative Agents & Large Language Models
  • Natural Language Processing & Text Mining
  • Knowledge Graph Construction & Graph Neural Networks
  • Big Data Mining & Complex Systems
  • Link Prediction & Causal Inference
  • Energy Technology Strategy & Scholar Influence Evaluation
