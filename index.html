🧐 About Me

I am Xu Binbin (徐彬彬), a Ph.D. student in Management Science and Engineering at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Institutes of Science and Development (GPA: 3.9/4), where I have been pursuing my doctoral degree since September 2022. Prior to this, I received my B.S. in Business Administration from Southwest Jiaotong University, School of Economics and Management (Chengdu, China) in June 2022.

Analysis of Power Data Elements and Technology Genealogy Construction

Developed an open-data and AI-driven research framework using PyTorch, BERT, and GRU models. Designed multi-scenario analysis frameworks to optimize R&D investment and model competitive dynamics, supporting data-driven strategic decision-making.

2022.05 – 2022.12 | State Grid Project

Developed multi-dimensional visualizations and knowledge graphs to track the evolution of hydrogen projects (2017–2022), revealing global trends and interconnections.
