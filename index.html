🧐 About Me

I am Xu Binbin (徐彬彬), a Ph.D. student in Management Science and Engineering at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Institutes of Science and Development (GPA: 3.9/4), where I have been pursuing my doctoral degree since September 2022. Prior to this, I received my B.S. in Business Administration from Southwest Jiaotong University, School of Economics and Management (Chengdu, China) in June 2022.

My research focuses on applying advanced AI and data-driven methods — including NLP, knowledge graph construction, graph neural networks, and large language models — to problems in energy strategy, technology intelligence, and scholarly influence evaluation.

🔬 Research Experiences

2025.10 – 2026.06 | Intern Project

SemRank: Semantic-Enhanced Scholar Influence Evaluation Platform

Spearheaded the R&D of a 5-stage framework optimizing PageRank via NetworkX with temporal and venue-quality factors. Synergized LLMs and Embeddings to automate topic clustering and classify citation intents (Zero-shot). Developed an interactive Web platform for visualization, currently supporting projects at Beihang University and overseas funding roadshows.

2025.05 – 2026.12 | State Grid Project

Analysis of Power Data Elements and Technology Genealogy Construction

Developed an AI-driven research framework using PyTorch, BERT, and GRU models. Transformed triple extraction into sequence labeling tasks, integrating part-of-speech priors and predicate masking for entity recognition. Enhanced relation extraction using syntactic analysis and SBERT-based semantic similarity from academic and patent literature.

2023.06 – 2024.12 | Huawei Shenzhen Project

Strategic Development Model for Energy Storage Enterprises

Utilized system dynamics, Haken model, ABM, and GCN to simulate digital energy strategy and resource allocation. Designed multi-scenario analysis frameworks to optimize R&D investment and model competitive dynamics.

2022.05 – 2022.12 | State Grid Project

Frontiers of Hydrogen Energy Technology

Applied NLP and text mining to analyze global hydrogen energy technologies across 14 thematic areas. Conducted comparative analysis of hydrogen strategies in China, the U.S., Japan, Germany, and the EU. Developed knowledge graphs tracking the evolution of hydrogen projects (2017–2022).
